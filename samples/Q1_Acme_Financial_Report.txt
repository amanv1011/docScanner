ACME CLOUD TECHNOLOGIES INC.
QUARTERLY FINANCIAL REPORT - Q1 2024
Report Date: April 15, 2024
Confidential - For Executive Review Only

1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
Acme Cloud delivered strong fiscal results for the first quarter of 2024, exceeding guidance across core enterprise software tiers. Robust demand for our unified workflow analytics platform fueled recurring revenue expansion across North America and EMEA.

2. FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE SUMMARY
- Total Quarterly Revenue: $12,400,000 USD (up 18.5% YoY)
- Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR): $48,200,000 USD
- GAAP Gross Margin: 74.2%
- Total Operating Expenses: $8,900,000 USD
  * Research & Development: $4,200,000 USD
  * Sales & Marketing: $3,100,000 USD
  * General & Administrative: $1,600,000 USD
- Operating Income: $1,250,000 USD
- Net Income: $820,000 USD
- Cash and Cash Equivalents: $34,500,000 USD

3. OPERATIONAL METRICS & KPI HIGHLIGHTS
- Full-Time Headcount: 142 employees (added 14 net new hires in Q1)
- Total Active Enterprise Customers: 412 accounts
- Net Retention Rate (NRR): 118%
- Enterprise Annual Churn Rate: 1.8% (Target guidance maintained below 1.5%)
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC): $14,200 USD per enterprise customer
- Average Contract Value (ACV): $86,000 USD

4. STRATEGIC INITIATIVES & PRODUCT ROADMAP
In Q1 2024, Acme completed rollout of the Multi-Tenant Security Gateway and began beta trials of the AI Decision Assistant with 25 pilot enterprise partners. 
Forward Guidance: The executive team reaffirms full-year ARR targets of $58.0M - $60.0M and projects Q2 operating margins remaining positive above 8%.
Audited and approved by: Marcus Vance, Chief Financial Officer.
