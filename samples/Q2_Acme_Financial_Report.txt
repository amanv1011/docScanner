ACME CLOUD TECHNOLOGIES INC.
QUARTERLY FINANCIAL REPORT - Q2 2024
Report Date: July 22, 2024
Confidential - For Executive Review Only

1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
Acme Cloud experienced rapid topline expansion during Q2 2024, alongside significant operational expenditure increases as we accelerated engineering hiring and expanded our global datacenter infrastructure to support generative AI workloads.

2. FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE SUMMARY
- Total Quarterly Revenue: $14,100,000 USD (up 13.7% QoQ from Q1's $12.4M)
- Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR): $54,800,000 USD
- GAAP Gross Margin: 71.5% (compressed by 270 bps due to cloud GPU infrastructure costs)
- Total Operating Expenses: $11,200,000 USD (spiked 25.8% compared to Q1)
  * Research & Development: $5,600,000 USD
  * Sales & Marketing: $3,900,000 USD
  * General & Administrative: $1,700,000 USD
- Operating Income: -$320,000 USD (Operating Loss)
- Net Income: -$410,000 USD (Diverged from Q1 profit of $820K)
- Cash and Cash Equivalents: $29,800,000 USD

3. OPERATIONAL METRICS & KPI HIGHLIGHTS
- Full-Time Headcount: 178 employees (added 36 net new hires in Q2)
- Total Active Enterprise Customers: 468 accounts
- Net Retention Rate (NRR): 112% (down 6 percentage points from Q1)
- Enterprise Annual Churn Rate: 3.2% (Discrepancy: exceeds Q1 management target ceiling of 1.5%)
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC): $18,176 USD (increased 28% QoQ)
- Average Contract Value (ACV): $89,500 USD

4. STRATEGIC INITIATIVES & RISK FACTORS
GPU server cluster procurement drove an unbudgeted $1.4M hardware amortization expense. Additionally, two legacy enterprise accounts terminated contracts early citing delayed regional data residency compliance in the EU.
Forward Guidance: Full-year ARR guidance revised to $54.0M - $56.0M (downgraded from prior $58M-$60M forecast). Operating profitability is not expected until Q4 2024.
Audited and approved by: Marcus Vance, Chief Financial Officer.
